Monotasker connects to your iOS Reminders and shows you a single task, picked at random from the list of your choice. Complete it, or shuffle for a different one. That's the whole app.

It's built for the moment a long list stops being useful and starts being a reason to avoid opening it. Monotasker doesn't ask you to sort, prioritize, or plan – it just hands you one thing to do next.

Requires iOS with access to Reminders.

<h2 id="privacy-and-terms">Privacy and terms</h2>

Monotasker reads and edits your Reminders through Apple's on-device Reminders API. That data never leaves your device except through Apple's own iCloud sync, which you control. Monotasker sends anonymous, cryptographically hashed telemetry via TelemetryDeck – aggregate counts of how often a feature gets used, never task content, personal information, or a device identifier – and nothing else. It doesn't run ads, sell data, or share data with third parties, and it isn't directed at children or intended for anyone under 13.
