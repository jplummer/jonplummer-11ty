Parker is a small IR time-of-flight sensor mounted in the garage that lights up once the car has pulled in exactly far enough – no more guessing, no more tennis ball on a string.

There's no app; it's a standalone piece of hardware with its own onboard software, updated over the air as it improves. It may become a printable kit, like Pointer-AR's physical pointer.
